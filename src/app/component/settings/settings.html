Display Settings

Shapes Mode

When enabled, color-coded groups display a unique shape in each cell, providing an alternative visual cue for players who may have difficulty distinguishing colors.

Show Row / Column Current Sum

Controls whether row and column headers display the current sum of selected cells in their upper-right corner.

Show Color Group Current Sum

Controls whether a color group's first cell displays the sum of the group's currently selected cells in the upper-right corner.

Show Scratch Pad

Controls whether the scratch pad displays next to the board. More information lives in the documentation.

Section Completion Animations

Plays a cascading glow across rows, columns, or groups when they meet their target value, signalling remaining cell can be cleared.

Auto Clear Cells

Unusable cells in completed sections will be automatically cleared and removed.

Tournament Mode

Allows incorrect moves, and doesn't warn when an incorrect move is made. Think of it like solving a crossword puzzle with pencil and paper — you may not notice a mistake until something else doesn't fit. When enabled, errors only become apparent when a row, column, or group's selections do not (or cannot) add up to their target value.

If a wrong selection is made, selecting the same cell again will undo the change.

The visibility icon beside the game number toggles the display of cleared (hidden) cells. While this could help spot which cells were selected or cleared incorrectly, the reality is that in tournament mode it's usually easier to just restart the board from the beginning - and this time, don't mess up!

Before disabling tournament mode while in the middle of a partially completed game, any errors on the board will need to be identified and fixed first, or the board started over. Disabling tournament mode will not fix any mistakes on a partially completed board.
